## Tips for Store Success

1. **Update regularly** - Even small updates show the app is maintained
2. **Respond to reviews** - Engage with your users
3. **Use all screenshot slots** - Show different features
4. **Add video preview** - Show the app in action
5. **Monitor metrics** - Track downloads, ratings, and uninstalls
6. **A/B test descriptions** - Try different wording
7. **Highlight what's unique** - 6 brushes, achievements, offline mode
8. **Show, don't just tell** - Screenshots should demonstrate features
