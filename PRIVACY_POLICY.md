# Privacy Policy for ArtJoy

**Last Updated: November 10, 2024**

## Introduction

ArtJoy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally

ArtJoy stores the following data locally on your device:
- Drawings created by you
- App settings and preferences
- Unlocked content and achievements
- Usage statistics (session count, drawings completed)

**Important:** All user data is stored locally on your device using Hive database. We do not collect, transmit, or store your drawings or personal data on our servers.

### Automatically Collected Information

When you use ArtJoy, we may automatically collect:
- Device information (model, OS version)
- App usage analytics (through AdMob)
- Ad interaction data (through Google AdMob)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the app functionality
- Save your drawings and preferences locally
- Display relevant advertisements
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Track unlocked content and achievements

## Third-Party Services

ArtJoy uses the following third-party services:

### Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:
- Device identifiers
- IP address
- Ad interaction data
- General location information

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties, except:
- To comply with legal obligations
- To protect our rights and safety
- With your explicit consent

## Children's Privacy

ArtJoy is designed for users of all ages, including children. We:
- Do NOT knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
- Store all data locally on the device only
- Use only child-safe advertising through AdMob
- Do NOT require account creation or personal information

Parents can delete all app data by uninstalling the app or using the "Clear All Drawings" feature in settings.

## Data Storage and Security

- All drawings and user data are stored locally on your device
- We use industry-standard encryption for data storage
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all local data

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your locally stored data
- Delete all your data (via app settings or uninstalling)
- Disable ads by purchasing Premium version
- Disable sound effects in settings

## Advertisements

ArtJoy displays advertisements through Google AdMob:
- Banner ads on home and gallery screens
- Interstitial ads (limited frequency)
- Rewarded ads (user-initiated only)
- Ads are NEVER shown during drawing

You can remove all ads by purchasing the Premium version.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
